**1. Physics: Matter.js 0.19 (MIT), vendored as `vendor/matter.min.js`**

  1. A soft body circle with a radial gradient (lighter top-left) and a 1.5 px darker outline.

- Bass: a sine with a soft envelope playing root on beat 1 and the fifth on the "and" of 3.
- Drums:
  - Kick: a soft sine dropping 110→45 Hz.
  - Snare: a bandpassed noise "brush" on beats 2 and 4.
  - Hats: highpassed noise ticks, swung 8ths at about 62%, with velocity humanised.
- Melody: a music-box triangle playing a 5-note F-pentatonic motif every other loop, with one note varied each time.
- Vinyl: a looped noise buffer with sparse random clicks and a faint 60 Hz hum, all at very low gain.
- Master chain: low-pass at about 1.6 kHz, with a 0.05 Hz LFO sweeping ±400 Hz, a slight wow (pitch LFO ±5 cents), and a gentle compressor.
- Audio starts on the first pointerdown or keydown. The mute toggle ramps master gain and is saved in localStorage.
- SFX: the drop is a soft sine "boop" (520→300 Hz). The merge is a pluck whose pitch climbs the pentatonic with kind k. The Mega merge is a rolled Fmaj9 arpeggio with a sparkle.

**6. Core loop and game over**
- Input:
  - Pointer events cover mouse and touch: move to aim, release to drop.
  - Arrow keys move the aim and space drops.
  - Cooldown is 500 ms, and the aim is clamped to the walls minus the cat's radius.
- Merging: equal-kind pairs from collision events are marked `merging` so a cat can't be used twice in one step. The new cat spawns at the midpoint with the averaged velocity.
  - Its radius grows from 70% to 100% over about 6 frames using `Body.scale`, so it doesn't suddenly shove its neighbours.
  - Merges also make a pop particle ring.
- Danger line at y = 100:
  - A cat counts only once it has touched something and only after 600 ms of grace since its drop.
  - If any counted cat's top edge stays above the line for 1.0 s, the game ends.
  - The line starts pulsing pink after 0.3 s.
- Game-over overlay: final score, best score (localStorage), a "new best!" tag and a big Restart button. Physics freezes and the cats keep breathing.
- Pause on `visibilitychange`. The canvas scales to fit at a fixed aspect ratio (letterboxed) and is DPR-aware.
